One of our promises was to always be transparent with each other, so I want to honor that one last time with this. You did everything in the most sincere manner and I can’t thank you enough for that. Love, at its core as we know it, is and should be selfless, and you’ve already given me so much to be grateful for. I didn’t think I’d ever be writing this, and I’ve started it more times than I want to admit. A little over a year doesn’t sound like long, but I’m not the same person I was before it. You were carefree and committed at the same time, and I didn’t know someone could be both. It changed what I think a relationship is supposed to feel like. It feels almost impossible to put into words the impact you’ve had on my life in just over a year. I’d never experienced something so beautiful yet so perspective-changing before and I can now see how I see myself, and how I see the beauty in someone else’s story. Although it was such a big part of our relationship, I want to keep the negatives out of this.

They say that if you love a flower so much, you shouldn’t pick it, because doing so removes it from the place where it was meant to thrive. That’s how I feel about us, although my greed got the better of me many times. I’ve come to realize that while I love the good things we’ve shared, my love for you also means I need to let you go and to honor the person you are, the life you’re living, and the path you’re on, even if we’re not doing it synchronously, alongside eachother.. There are times I mentioned that I’m glad I met you-I won’t and can’t take it back because they were true then and remain true now. You’ve shown me what it means to have quality not just in how you carry yourself but in the way you make people feel seen, valued, and understood. Because of you, my standards have been raised in the best way possible. I’ll never settle for anything less than the depth and authenticity you brought into my life. You’ve become the measure against which I’ll hold every future connection and I’ll never regret the late nights we shared on the phone when we first met as well as the voice that brought peace to my mornings. We found our own language across states and with the little jokes only we understood. You’ve reshaped how I understand love, not necessarily as something to grasp tightly but as something to hold with gratitude even when it’s fleeting.

I've been asking myself why the most influential person I've ever met has to be the one I need to take a step back from. But maybe it’s because loving you has shown me what it means to want the best for someone, even if it means letting them go. I think it’s appropriate to quote one of ryan’s lines in this case: <em>We are creatures of possibility, forever haunted by the ghosts of what might have been. In love, this is especially true—it's not the memory of what was said or done that lingers, but the words we never spoke, the glances we missed, the love we never fully gave. What we think about more isn’t what we did, but what we’ll never know—if this was the ending we were meant to have or if we’re always standing on the brink of another.</em> I feel that your presence in my life, as well as your absence to come, is nothing short of a blessing. Having you taught me about love, strength, and vulnerability, and losing you is teaching me how to carry those lessons forward with grace. I’ll be praying for you every day with gratitude for what we had even though we both could’ve appreciated more good times, and for the path that God has for you. And I’ll keep praying for you so that I don’t forget you. Oftentimes when we think of what comes after grief- we think of happiness.. the supposed opposite.. But I’ve learned that the opposite of grief is forgetting. And forgetting can be a negligent action especially in a case where a part of who I am now is who I became when I was with you.

Our story will always be part of who I am. The time we shared, though relatively short, has left a mark on my life that I will carry with me. I hope you continue to shine in the beautiful, graceful way you always have, and I’ll always be rooting for your happiness in everything you do. I can’t wait to see what the future holds for you and I have such a strong conviction you’re going to do great things. Please, keep being you, keep giving everyone your best and don’t lose sight of the kindness you carry. Thank you for being the light you were to me, even if only for a year.
