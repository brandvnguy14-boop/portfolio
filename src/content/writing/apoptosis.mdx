---
title: "Apoptosis"
date: 2024-09-03
type: poetry
dek: ""
---

The cell that chooses its own ending
does not rupture—
it folds inward, signals
its own dismemberment.

A quiet protocol.
The neighbors clean it up
without inflammation,
without scar.

---

I have been thinking about the deaths
that look like nothing,
the ones that leave no wreckage,
no raised tissue at the site.

The body has a name for this.
It calls it programmed.
As if intention
were the same as grace.
